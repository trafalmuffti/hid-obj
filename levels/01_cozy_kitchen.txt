LEVEL 01: THE COZY KITCHEN
==========================
Role: Tutorial level. Teaches the core loop (look, find, tap).
Difficulty: 1/10   Objects to find: 8   Suggested time: none (untimed)   Hints: unlimited, no cooldown

SCENE
A bright, tidy farmhouse kitchen in morning light. One fixed camera, no scrolling.
Warm wood cabinets, cream walls, a checkered floor, a window over the sink showing a garden.
Moderate clutter: about 40 background props, all large and clearly drawn.

VISUAL / CONTRAST RULES FOR THIS LEVEL
- Background palette is warm and muted (creams, tans, soft browns).
- Every target uses a saturated color that appears nowhere else in the background
  (teal, cherry red, sunflower yellow). Dark outline on every target.
- No target is occluded by more than 0%. Everything is fully visible.
- Minimum target size: 6% of screen width.

TARGET OBJECTS
1. Red teapot            - on the stove, left burner. Red against black stovetop.
2. Yellow rubber duck    - beside the sink faucet. Yellow against white porcelain.
3. Blue key              - hanging on a hook by the door. Blue against cream wall.
4. Green apple           - in the fruit bowl, amid orange and yellow fruit.
5. Purple whisk handle   - in the utensil crock with brown wooden spoons.
6. Silver cat figurine   - on the windowsill, silhouetted against the garden.
7. Pink cupcake          - on the cooling rack, among plain brown cookies.
8. White egg with blue   - in an egg carton, among plain white eggs
   polka dots              (the only decorated one).

RED HERRINGS (look similar, are not targets, never clickable as targets)
- A red coffee pot on the counter (confuses with the red teapot; different shape, has a spout lid).
- A yellow sponge by the sink (same yellow as the duck, but rectangular).
- A blue ceramic jug (same blue as the key).
- Red apples and a green pear in the same bowl as the green apple.
- A gray cat photo on the fridge (resembles the silver cat figurine).
- Plain egg carton on the shelf with no dotted egg.

MISCLICK BEHAVIOR
Gentle: a soft "bonk" sound, no penalty, the herring wobbles to show it is just scenery.

NOTES FOR BUILD
- Item list displayed as silhouettes with labels (tutorial teaches reading the list).
- First object found triggers a short tooltip explaining hint button.
