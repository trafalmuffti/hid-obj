LEVEL 07: THE CLOCKMAKER'S WORKSHOP
===================================
Role: Introduces moving targets and timing.
Difficulty: 6/10   Objects to find: 15   Suggested time: 8:00   Hints: 3, 40s recharge

SCENE
A cramped workshop crowded with clocks of every size, gears, springs, tools, a workbench with a
half-built automaton, shelves of jars, pendulums swinging, a cuckoo clock. Warm amber light.

VISUAL / CONTRAST RULES
- Background is brown, bronze and amber; targets use cool or white tones, or are bright
  enamel colors, so they separate from the metal.
- Moving elements (pendulums, cuckoo bird) are decorative. Only 2 targets move, and each
  stays on screen and in view at least 3 out of every 4 seconds.
- Moving targets are marked in the list with a small "in motion" icon.

TARGET OBJECTS
1. Blue enamel clock face - on the wall, among brass and wooden faces.
2. Red cuckoo bird        - emerges from the cuckoo clock on the hour (MOVING).
3. Silver magnifying glass - on the bench, lens catching light.
4. Green felt pouch       - on a hook, tied with a yellow string.
5. White china teacup     - on a shelf among brown jars.
6. Gold key (winding)     - in the lock of a grandfather clock.
7. Pink gear              - a single pink-painted gear in a bin of brass gears.
8. Purple glove           - draped on a vise.
9. Orange mouse           - runs along the floor baseboard (MOVING), loops every 8 seconds.
10. Black top-hat pendulum- a pendulum weight shaped like a hat among round bobs.
11. Teal ink bottle       - at the desk edge, cork stopper.
12. Yellow pencil         - lying on a blueprint, bright against blue paper.
13. Glass monocle         - hanging on a chain from a shelf.
14. Red wax seal          - on a letter on the desk.
15. Ivory music box       - on a high shelf, small ballerina figure inside.

RED HERRINGS
- Blue and white painted plates; other clock faces with numbers in blue.
- A red toy bird on the mantelpiece, a red bird painted on a calendar.
- Brass magnifying frames without lenses, round mirrors, a silver spoon.
- Many pouches in brown leather, a green wallet.
- Teacups in other colors; white eggs in a bowl; white jugs.
- Brass keys of the same shape, a silver key on the floor.
- Pink erasers, pink thread spools.
- Brown mice figurines, a gray cat statue (a real cat sleeps in a corner).
- Black top hats on a coat stand; round brass bobs.
- Ink bottles in black and brown; green glass vials.
- Yellow pencils in a jar (the target is the single one lying on the blueprint).

MISCLICK BEHAVIOR
Tick sound, no penalty.

NOTES FOR BUILD
- Moving targets: clicking anywhere on the sprite counts; hitbox is enlarged by 25%.
