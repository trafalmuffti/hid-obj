LEVEL 08: THE GRAND TRAIN STATION
=================================
Role: Wide scrolling scene with a story thread. Found items reveal a mini-mystery
      (a lost traveler's belongings).
Difficulty: 7/10   Objects to find: 16   Suggested time: 9:00   Hints: 2, 45s recharge

SCENE
A vast steel-and-glass station hall in 1920s style. Departure boards, a clock tower,
steam engines, platforms, luggage carts, travelers, kiosks, pigeons. Scene is 3 screens wide.
Gray steel, sepia stone, with a few colorful posters and signs.

VISUAL / CONTRAST RULES
- Hall is mostly gray/sepia; targets use saturated color or a distinct bright material.
- Dense crowd, but crowd uses muted coats; none match target colors exactly.
- 5 to 6 targets per screen section. A target may be 30% hidden behind a crowd member,
  but the identifying feature (shape or color) stays visible.
