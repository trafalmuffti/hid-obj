LEVEL 10: THE GRAND LIBRARY (FINALE)
====================================
Role: Capstone. Reuses motifs from levels 1 to 9 as callbacks. Rewards mastery of every skill taught.
Difficulty: 9/10   Objects to find: 20   Suggested time: 12:00   Hints: 2, 60s recharge

SCENE
An enormous multi-floor library: balconies, rolling ladders, a glass dome, a reading hall,
globes, statues, stained-glass windows, a fireplace, moving dust motes, a hidden-door bookcase.
Scene is 3 screens wide and 2 tall, with scroll and zoom (1x to 2x). Warm sunlit oak with
jewel-toned stained-glass light patches.

VISUAL / CONTRAST RULES
- Every target obeys the same readability promise as earlier levels: closed silhouette, unique
  hue or value within local area, no more than 30% occlusion, never in full shadow.
- Stained-glass light patches are decorative and only brighten targets.
- Each target is a callback to a previous level, shown in the item list with a tiny level badge.
- Targets are spread across 6 zones (each ground floor wing, mezzanine, dome, reading hall,
  basement stairs, fireplace nook) with 3 to 4 per zone.
