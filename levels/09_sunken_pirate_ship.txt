LEVEL 09: THE SUNKEN PIRATE SHIP
================================
Role: Hard late-game level using underwater color loss, tight clutter, and look-alike sets.
Difficulty: 8/10   Objects to find: 16   Suggested time: 9:00   Hints: 2, 50s recharge

SCENE
The wreck of a galleon on the seabed. Broken masts, barnacled cannons, treasure chests, coral,
fish schools, anchor chains, sunken barrels, skeletons, rays of light from above.
Blue-green cast, silt particles.

VISUAL / CONTRAST RULES
- Underwater tint reduces reds, so reds are drawn slightly brighter/more orange than in earlier levels.
  Targets still keep a hard outline and a color unique within a 15% screen radius.
- Light shafts brighten targets, never darken them. No fog covering any target.
- Where a target's color is close to the water tint, its shape must be crisp and man-made.
- Fish schools move in front of the scene but are on a layer behind targets' clickability and
  never fully cover a target for more than 1 second.

TARGET OBJECTS
1. Gold doubloon          - half-buried in sand beside a chest.
2. Red coral crown        - on a skull, red coral against pale bone.
3. Silver cutlass         - stuck upright in the deck planks.
4. Green bottle with map  - tucked into a barrel crack, scroll visible inside.
5. Pink conch shell       - on the seabed, among white shells.
6. Yellow pufferfish      - hiding in the cannon barrel, spines out.
7. Purple octopus tentacle - curled around the anchor.
8. Black spyglass         - lying on the captain's wheel, brass rings.
9. White pearl in oyster  - open oyster among closed ones.
10. Orange compass        - hanging from the mast, brass and orange.
11. Brown parrot feather  - floating near the crow's nest.
12. Blue sapphire ring    - on a skeleton finger.
13. Gold goblet           - on a table in the captain's cabin.
14. Teal key              - in a lock of a chest, other chests unlocked.
15. Red anchor tattoo flag - torn pirate flag on a snagged rope.
16. Bronze bell           - hung under the bow, half covered with coral but ringing shape visible.

RED HERRINGS
- Gold coins everywhere in the open chest (only the single half-buried doubloon outside counts).
- Many corals in pink and red; a red starfish; a red fish.
- Other swords: rusty sabers, silver fish shaped like blades.
- Green bottles and seaweed; clear empty bottles.
- Pink shells and pink sea anemones.
- Yellow fish of other types (sea butterflies, angelfish), yellow sponge coral.
- Purple sea urchins, purple anemone, other tentacles of an eel.
- Black telescopes of other kinds: a black pipe, a black bone.
- White pearls in nearby clam shells (already empty or closed), white bone, white pebbles.
- Other brass compasses, brass lamps, brass portholes.
- Feathers painted on a figurehead.
- Blue gems in a necklace in a chest, blue fish.
- Many goblets and cups of silver/pewter.
- Many keys: rusty, iron, gold-colored; a key-shaped coral.
- Several flags: black Jolly Roger, striped sails.

MISCLICK BEHAVIOR
Bubble pop. After 8 misclicks in a row, a hint button pulses.

NOTES FOR BUILD
- Light shafts sweep slowly. Test that no shaft or fish ever fully hides a target.
