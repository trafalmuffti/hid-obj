LEVEL 02: THE DUSTY ATTIC
=========================
Role: Introduces layered clutter and shape-based searching.
Difficulty: 2/10   Objects to find: 10   Suggested time: 5:00 (soft, no fail)   Hints: 3, 20s recharge

SCENE
A sloped-roof attic lit by a single round window and a dangling bulb.
Trunks, covered furniture, hanging garments, a dressmaker's mannequin, boxes, a rocking horse.
Cool gray-blue shadows with a warm yellow pool of light around the bulb.

VISUAL / CONTRAST RULES
- Background is desaturated gray-blue and brown. Targets are the only warm, saturated or
  brightly lit items, or are placed inside the warm light pool.
- Targets may overlap background props by up to 15% of their area but their
  silhouette remains identifiable.
- Light rim highlight on every target (suggests dust catching light).

TARGET OBJECTS
1. Brass trumpet          - leaning against a trunk, glinting under the bulb.
2. Red mitten             - on a coat hanger among gray coats.
3. Porcelain doll head    - on a shelf, white face against dark wood.
4. Orange lantern         - hung from a rafter, brightest item on screen.
5. Green glass bottle     - behind a bannister post, 10% overlap.
6. Wind-up toy robot      - on the floor, tin silver and blue with red key.
7. Gold pocket watch      - half-draped over a trunk lid, chain visible.
8. Pink ballet slipper    - tucked in an open suitcase of gray clothes.
9. Yellow umbrella        - hanging from a beam among black umbrellas.
10. Teal paint tin        - on a stack of boxes, with a drip down its side.

RED HERRINGS
- A brass tuba bell and a golden candlestick (confuse with the trumpet and watch).
- Pink and red gloves in a different drawer; one red sock (confuses with the mitten).
- A gray toy robot head inside a box, a pile of bolts.
- Two other porcelain doll bodies without heads (heads are decoys, not the target head).
- Black umbrellas of identical shape; one yellow raincoat (same yellow, wrong item).
- Several other paint tins in dull colors; one teal flowerpot.
- A second lantern, unlit and dark, on the floor.

MISCLICK BEHAVIOR
Soft thud. Three misclicks within 5 seconds causes a brief dust-cloud flash (no penalty).

NOTES FOR BUILD
- Teach players to scan along light vs. shadow regions.
- Lightbulb gently swings to move the shadows (cosmetic only, never hides targets).
